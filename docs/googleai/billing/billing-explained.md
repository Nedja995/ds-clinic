### 1. The meaning of the first icon item in the table
The small colored shapes next to the SKU names (e.g., the blue circle, orange diamond, green inverted triangle) are **legend markers**.

They do not represent anything specific about the technology or the AI model itself. Instead, they are dynamically generated by the Google Cloud Console to correspond to the visual chart (usually a line graph or bar chart) that sits just above this table. If you were to scroll up slightly on that webpage, you would see a graph. The line or bar showing the cost for "Generate_content text output token count for gemini 3 pro short" would be represented by that exact blue circle color, allowing you to easily map the data in the table to the visual graph.
